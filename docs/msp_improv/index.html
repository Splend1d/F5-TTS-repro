MSP-IMPROV listening samples

Emotional zero-shot TTS on MSP-IMPROV. Each target is a real recording by an actor. The model gets a prompt from the same speaker in the same emotion saying something else, plus the target text. The question is whether the output carries that emotion.

Generation: EMA weights, seed 0, 32 Euler steps, CFG 2, sway sampling −1. The output length comes from F5-TTS's prompt-rate rule (no duration model). Under each clip: emotion2vec+ large's top class among angry, happy, sad and neutral, with its share of those four. It is green when it matches the row's emotion.

Rows were drawn at random (seed ) under coverage rules: distinct speakers and sentences, both genders, read and in-scene clips, short to long lines. They were not chosen by how they sound.

Built by repro/make_msp_demo.py from the test sets of repro/build_msp_emo.py and the outputs of repro/msp_emo_eval.sbatch. Audio from the MSP-IMPROV corpus (Busso et al., 2017) and voices cloned from it, used under the corpus license.
